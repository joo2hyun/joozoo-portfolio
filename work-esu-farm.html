ESU Farm

In-house design at a New Jersey produce importer: building out Tommy's Farm, the company's own fruit brand, and taking ESU to the Global Produce & Floral Show.

Year
2026
Client
ESU Farm LLC
Brands
Tommy's Farm (in-house), ESU (company)
Category
Branding, Marketing
Role
Graphic Designer & Marketing Associate

Overview

One fruit brand, one company, one show floor.

ESU sources produce from growers abroad and distributes it across the US. Tommy's Farm is its consumer brand — shine muscat, pomegranate and mandarins carried by a farm-boy mascot — and I built it out from the box to the website. In October 2026 both names went to the Global Produce & Floral Show in Orlando, so the show work runs on two voices: ESU speaking to buyers as a sourcing partner, Tommy's Farm speaking as the product on the shelf.

In-house brand

Tommy's Farm

ESU's own produce brand. A navy-and-cream premium line for shine muscat alongside brighter, character-led packs for the rest of the range, tied together by one mascot and one website.

Shine Muscat 1lb box

01 — Packaging

Four 1lb (454g) clamshells to a case. Deep navy and cream with a line-drawn vineyard wrapping all four sides, gold script for “Premium”, and a matte finish so the fruit carries the colour. The tray and clamshell label repeat the same navy so a single pack still reads as the box it came from.

Product one-pagers

02 — Sales collateral

Hover or tap a card to see the back.

A 5×7 sheet per product for buyers: one flat colour per fruit, a chunky headline, the retail pack front and centre, and a QR code to tommysfarm.us. Same grid on all four, so the set reads as a range when fanned out on a table.

tommysfarm.us

03 — Website

A one-page brand site: the season's picks, a four-step sourcing story told by the mascot, a scroll-driven farm road scene and a wholesale contact form — cream, tomato red and farm green, built mobile-first. Visit the site ↗

Trade show — Orlando, Oct 14–16, 2026

Global Produce & Floral Show

ESU's booth #2091 at the IFPA show. The company name went on the build and the buyer material; the Tommy's Farm range and mascot filled the product wall, the sampling counter and the brand's own social feed.

Booth

04 — 20×20 ft island

A wood-frame island: a hanging ESU ring sign visible across the hall, a Global Sourcing banner on the aisle side, five product panels behind a bar counter, and a lit ESU × Tommy's Farm counter at the corner for sampling.

Product banner wall

05 — Booth graphics

One panel per product, each in its box's own colour, the name set vertically so it reads from down the aisle, and a shelf niche cut in to hold the real box.

Bi-fold brochure

06 — Print
Click the brochure to open or close it.

The leave-behind for buyers: ESU and its brands on the outside, then the inside split between the Tommy's Farm range — season and pack size under each product — and a Seoul Shine page for the premium shine muscat.

Show ads

07 — Print & digital

One ad in two sizes for The Produce News: a 10×15 print page and a 300×250 web banner. The same fruit close-up and three-word headline carry across; the booth number and a “Click to schedule” button do the work in the digital cut.

Global Sourcing banner

08 — Booth banner, 47.5×115.7 in

The tall banner on the booth's aisle side: where ESU sources from, the boxes it ships, a season chart, and how it reaches the US — the company pitch in one standing read.

Show social

09 — Reels, two accounts

The same show, told twice. ESU's feed stayed live-action and buyer-facing — the trip, opening day, the meetings. Tommy's Farm ran a character-led countdown from the farm to the booth. Lined up on one date axis so the two voices read side by side.
