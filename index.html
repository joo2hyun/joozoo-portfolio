Hi, I'm Joohyun Kim — a graphic and visual designer based in New York. I studied at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago, where I developed a rigorous eye for craft and concept.

I've worked across agency, nonprofit, and cultural institutions — with organizations like Williams Lea, Harris Theater, SAIC, Esri, and Reingold — bringing visual clarity to complex communication challenges.

My work spans brand identity, editorial design, print, and digital. Every project starts with listening and ends with something that couldn't have been made for anyone else.
